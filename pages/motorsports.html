The Program

Since winter 2023, I've been competing in 24 Hours of Lemons—one of the most addictive, humbling, and rewarding racing series there is. The format is simple: $500 budget ceiling, vintage cars, and enough time to find out what you're really made of.

What started as curiosity turned into something deeper. Racing teaches you things that engineering textbooks can't: systems thinking under chaos, decision-making with incomplete information, and the weight of real consequences. You learn that strategy beats horsepower, consistency beats ego, and that a team approach works better than individual heroics.

The Car: 2001 BMW 530i

A Bavarian luxury sedan transformed into a purpose-built race car. The 530i platform has proven itself capable and reliable over multiple 24-hour endurance events. We've progressively refined the setup, suspension geometry, and reliability systems to compete at the highest level of our class.

Key Modifications: Upgraded suspension, braking system optimization, safety systems, fuel management, and detailed reliability improvements. Every change serves a purpose—removing weight, improving balance, or increasing durability.
